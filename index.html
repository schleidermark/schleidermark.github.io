Mark rclone

Private, non-commercial OAuth client used exclusively by its owner to mount his own Google Drive on his personal Linux desktop via the open-source tool rclone. It is not offered to, or intended for, any other users.

Privacy Policy

This application accesses Google Drive data only for the account of its owner, who authorizes it himself. No data is collected, stored, shared, sold or transferred to any third party. All data stays between Google's servers and the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer only.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
